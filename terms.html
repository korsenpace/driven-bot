Terms of Service — Drivent

Last updated: September 2026

Drivent is a personal, self-built SMS assistant used to manage one individual's training schedule. It is not a commercial product and has no public sign-up.

Service description

Drivent sends daily training check-ins (morning and evening) and responds to text messages with training-related information, based on data the sole user maintains in the application.

Message frequency

Approximately twice daily, plus reply messages as needed. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for support.
