Privacy Policy — Drivent

Last updated: September 2026

Drivent is a personal training assistant built for private, individual use. This policy describes how it handles the single mobile phone number it communicates with.

What information is collected

Drivent stores basic training data (workout schedule, run distances, body weight logs, and check-in notes) and the mobile phone number it texts. No other personal data is collected.

Mobile number use

Your mobile phone number will not be shared or sold to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. It is used solely to send and receive the personal training messages described in this service.

Message frequency

Messages are sent approximately twice per day (a morning training check-in and an evening wind-down check-in), plus replies to any messages you send.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for support.

Contact

This is a personal, self-built application with a single user and no public sign-up. There is no third-party data sharing of any kind.
